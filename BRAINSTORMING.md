# Brainstorming

I think that we may have gone too far too fast, without thinking about the vision. What do I want this to have, to look
like? What features and growth potential do I want?

First, let's think about the purpose of the portfolio. For those who are skimming, it should look good and tell a story
about myself without being overwhelming or confusing. For those who are interested in my thought and design process,
there should be a deeper dive into each project. While I am not a "frontend guy", I should do my best to have a coherent
design language throughout. I have this vision of off-black and white, clean lines but not too clinical, like lightly
aged paper. For the main font, I'll want something nice and geometrical, but for headings and such it should be a thin
serif font, clean but with character. Variety of color will really come from the content itself, I just need to make
sure that the page itself is fast and responsive and looks decent.

On the main page, I want to have my name, a little blurb, three or so featured projects, a nice button that links to a
separate projects page, and then below that various external links and contact info. The featured projects should
rearrange themselves based on the screen size, of course. The projects should be in nice clickable cards, with the
title, thumbnail, desctiption, tags, and buttons to see the github or live site.

On the main projects page, I want it to be a vertical, reverse-chronological list of all the projects based on the start
date. Each one should show as a thumbnail on the right of the timeline. The title, summary, and
tags should be to the right of this thumbnail, contained by it vertically. The whole thing should be a link to the
page, but there should also maybe be an arrow or go to page link (think about this for the cards). On the left of the
timeline there should be years placed based on the project start dates, perhaps with an aligning dot/circle on the
timeline. Doesn't need to be perfectly calculated, evenly spaced between projects might even be better. Consider how to
treat WIPs in this context.

Anyways, for the project pages themselves it will be mainly text, images, and diagrams. In the future I may add an
embedded video or interactive component though. The think to consider is where to put tags, date info, links, etc. Some
projects are complicated because I started them, put them down, then revived them years later. And it's worth
considering what to do with related projects. Side panel? Foot of the page? What should I do with tags, on the project
page or in general? Should they be interactive or just indicators of domain. Not all projetcs on the timeline are
created equal, should I have a way to differentiate projects aside from the front page featuring.

Is there room for a gallery page that displays a random mix of images from all projects together, and maybe links to
them from there? What about tabs and a navbar? What if home/projects/gallery are all just different way to peruse what
I've got and find projects? Maybe I could toss in an interactive search feature that goes based on frontmatter? And if I
have a navbar, do I put my name in it the same or different than I do on the main home page? Should I have the navbar
behave different on the home page? Transitions? What personal/contact info is safe or reasonable to provide and what's
not?
